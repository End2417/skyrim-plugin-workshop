What it does

Edit any plugin

Browse every record in a .esp or .esm, search by FormID or Editor ID, and change values in readable fields instead of raw bytes. Records you don’t touch save byte-for-byte identical.

Extract only your change

Edited a sword in Skyrim.esm? Pick it and save it alone as a new .esp that overrides the original. Your edit becomes a mod, and Skyrim.esm stays untouched.

Private and offline

Files are opened and saved on your phone. The app needs no account and never uploads your files. Saved plugins go to Documents/ESP Editor.

Install on Android

  1. Open the latest release on your phone and download ESP-Editor.apk.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open ESP Editor.
  4. Tap Open and choose a plugin. Make your edits, then use Save for the whole file or Extract for
