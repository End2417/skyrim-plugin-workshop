IronSword WEAP:00012EB7 Value 25 Damage 712 Weight 9.0 Skyrim_Changes.esp 1 record · master: Skyrim.esm What it does Edit any plugin Browse every record in a .esp or .esm, search by FormID or Editor ID, and change values in readable fields instead of raw bytes. Records you don’t touch save byte-for-byte identical. Extract only your change Edited a sword in Skyrim.esm? Pick it and save it alone as a new .esp that overrides the original. Your edit becomes a mod, and Skyrim.esm stays untouched. Private and offline Files are opened and saved on your phone. The app needs no account and never uploads your files. Saved plugins go to Documents/ESP Editor. Install on Android Open the latest release on your phone and download ESP-Editor.apk. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps. Tap Install, then open ESP Editor. Tap Open and choose a plugin. Make your edits, then use Save for the whole file or Extract for just your changes. Find saved files in your Files app under Documents/ESP Editor, then copy them into Skyrim’s Data folder.
